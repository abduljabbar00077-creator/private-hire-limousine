Terms and Conditions

These general terms describe how enquiries and quotations are handled through Private Hire Limousine. Specific accepted quotations or written booking confirmations may contain additional journey details.

Enquiries and quotations

Customers should provide accurate journey information, including pickup and destination, date, time, passenger count and vehicle requirements. A request for a quotation is not a confirmed booking.

Booking confirmation

A journey is treated as confirmed only after the quotation and journey details have been accepted and confirmation has been communicated. Availability and pricing may depend on the requested date, route, duration, vehicle and other requirements.

Changes and cancellations

Please contact us as early as possible if you need to change or cancel a requested journey. Any applicable waiting, cancellation or amendment terms should be confirmed in the quotation or booking confirmation before the journey.

Journey information

Customers are responsible for providing correct pickup and destination details and allowing sufficient time for their journey. Travel times may be affected by traffic, road conditions, access restrictions and events.

Contact

For clarification before accepting a quotation, contact contact@privatehirelimousine.com or WhatsApp us.

These general terms should be reviewed by the business for operational accuracy and legal compliance before publication as final terms.
