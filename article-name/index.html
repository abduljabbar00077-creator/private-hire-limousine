Burj Khalifa Dubai: A Must-Visit Landmark on Your Next UAE Trip

There are some places you simply have to see when visiting Dubai, and Burj Khalifa is certainly one of them. Whether you're arriving in the city for the first time or have visited before, the sight of this remarkable building is hard to forget.

Standing 828 metres tall, Burj Khalifa is the world's tallest building and one of the most recognisable landmarks in the United Arab Emirates. Its striking design, panoramic views and location in the heart of Downtown Dubai make it a popular stop for visitors from around the world.

But a visit is about more than looking up at a tall building. The surrounding neighbourhood has plenty to see, so it's easy to turn a quick stop into a relaxed afternoon or evening out.

Burj Khalifa is the centrepiece of the Downtown Dubai skyline.

What makes Burj Khalifa so special?

The first thing you notice when you approach Burj Khalifa is its scale. Seeing it from street level gives you a very different impression from looking at photographs. The tower's design draws on elements of Islamic architecture, while its tapering form gives it a distinctive profile against the Dubai skyline.

One of the most popular experiences is visiting the At The Top observation decks on Levels 124 and 125. From there, visitors can look across the city, the coastline and the desert beyond. There is also a premium observation experience on Level 148.

If photography is part of your plan, consider booking around sunset. The light changes quickly, and watching the city move from daylight into evening can be a highlight of the visit. Check ticket availability and entry details before you go, as popular time slots can sell out.

Explore more than just Burj Khalifa

One of the best things about visiting Burj Khalifa is how much there is to do nearby. You don't need to travel far to find shopping, restaurants and some of Dubai's best-known city views.

Dubai Mall

Right beside the tower, Dubai Mall is much more than a shopping centre. You can browse international brands, choose from a wide range of restaurants or spend time at its family attractions. Even if shopping isn't on your list, it's worth allowing time to explore.

The Dubai Fountain

Outside the mall, the Dubai Fountain draws crowds with its choreographed water, music and light performances. The atmosphere is especially lively in the evening, when the surrounding buildings are illuminated.

Souk Al Bahar

A short walk away, Souk Al Bahar offers an Arabian-inspired setting with cafés, restaurants and views across the water towards Burj Khalifa. It's a pleasant place to pause after sightseeing.

The Downtown area is worth exploring after visiting the observation decks.

When is the best time to visit?

Burj Khalifa can be visited throughout the day, and the best time depends on the kind of experience you're after. Morning visits can suit travellers who prefer to start early. The afternoon is useful if you want to combine the tower with Dubai Mall and nearby attractions.

Sunset is particularly popular because you can see the city in daylight and watch the skyline light up. It is also one of the busiest periods, so booking tickets ahead of time is a good idea. Check the official ticketing website for current timings, prices and visitor information.

Travelling from Abu Dhabi to Burj Khalifa

If you're staying in Abu Dhabi, a day trip to Burj Khalifa is a straightforward way to add Dubai to your UAE itinerary. The drive between the two cities takes planning, particularly if you want to visit more than one attraction.

Some visitors choose public transport, while others prefer a taxi or private transportation. A pre-arranged private journey can be useful if you are travelling with family, carrying luggage or hoping to visit several places on your own schedule. Journey time varies with traffic, the pickup point and the time of day, so allow some flexibility.

A small planning tip: Decide which attractions matter most before you set off. Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and the fountain area can easily take up several hours, especially if you plan to visit an observation deck.

Other places to include in your Dubai visit

If you have more time, you could add another part of the city to your itinerary. Palm Jumeirah is known for its waterfront setting and resort hotels. Dubai Marina is a lively district for a walk beside the water, while the Museum of the Future is worth seeing for its distinctive architecture. Jumeirah Beach is another option if you'd like a coastal stop and views of Burj Al Arab.

There is no need to squeeze everything into one day. Choosing a few places that interest you will usually make the trip more enjoyable than rushing between a long list of stops.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Burj Khalifa located?

Burj Khalifa is in Downtown Dubai, next to Dubai Mall, in the United Arab Emirates.

How tall is Burj Khalifa?

Burj Khalifa is 828 metres tall and is recognised as the world's tallest building.

Can visitors go inside Burj Khalifa?

Yes. Visitors can buy tickets for the At The Top observation experiences. Check the official website for current access, prices and available time slots.

How much time should I allow for a visit?

Allow a few hours for the observation deck and some time to explore the surrounding Downtown area. Your total visit will depend on ticket time, queues and the attractions you choose.

Can I travel from Abu Dhabi to Burj Khalifa by private car?

Yes. You can travel by private car or arrange a private chauffeur transfer. Allow extra time for traffic and confirm your pickup and drop-off details in advance.

Is Burj Khalifa worth visiting?

For many visitors, yes. The observation decks, architecture and nearby Downtown attractions make it one of Dubai's best-known visitor experiences.
