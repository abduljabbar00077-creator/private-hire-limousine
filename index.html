About Us

Private Hire Limousine is a chauffeur service for Abu Dhabi and Dubai, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

We started with a simple idea: a journey should feel calm from the first message to the final drop-off. Whether you are landing after a long flight, travelling between meetings, or arriving at an event, your chauffeur is there on time, and the car is clean and ready.

We work with visitors, residents, hotels and businesses. Each trip is quoted clearly before you confirm, and you can reach a real person by WhatsApp or phone at any hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends an enquiry. A booking is confirmed only when we reply with a quotation and you accept it.

Which areas do you serve?

Abu Dhabi and Dubai, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How do airport pickups work?

Add your flight number in the form so your chauffeur can track arrival. We are an independent company with no official airport affiliation.

How is the price set?

Guide prices in AED are listed on this page. You receive a written quotation before you confirm, and it may differ for extra stops, waiting time or special requests.

Can I change or cancel?

Contact us as early as possible. Any cancellation terms are confirmed in your written quotation.
