Our Formula 1 Abu Dhabi Transportation Services

1. Airport Transfers During Formula 1 Weekend

Arriving in Abu Dhabi for the Grand Prix? Arrange an airport pickup from Zayed International Airport to your hotel, Yas Island accommodation or another destination in the city. When enquiring, share your flight number, arrival time, passenger count and luggage requirements so the journey can be quoted accurately.

2. Hotel Transfers to Yas Marina Circuit

If you are staying in Abu Dhabi or Dubai, you can request a private transfer to the Yas Marina Circuit area. Hotel pickups can be discussed in advance, with the exact drop-off point depending on event traffic plans, road closures and official access arrangements.

3. Race-Day Private Chauffeur

For qualifying sessions and race day, a private driver can provide a planned pickup and a return journey arranged around your schedule. Tell us your preferred departure time and where you would like to be collected after the event. Circuit access and pickup points are controlled by event organisers, so your driver may need to use a designated location nearby.

4. Full-Day Chauffeur Service

If your plans include several destinations, meetings or hospitality activities, ask about a full-day chauffeur arrangement. Share your itinerary and expected hours so we can advise on availability and provide a quotation for the requested service.

5. Corporate and VIP Guest Transportation

The Grand Prix weekend is also a busy period for corporate hospitality, business meetings and visiting executives. Private Hire Limousine can discuss transportation arrangements for corporate guests and coordinated pickups. For multiple vehicles or several pickup locations, provide the schedule as early as possible.

6. Evening and After-Party Transfers

There is more to the Grand Prix weekend than the race itself. If you plan to attend a concert, dinner or evening event, you can request a private return transfer. Include the venue, expected finish time and preferred drop-off location when asking for a quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide transfers to Yas Marina Circuit during Formula 1?

Yes, you can enquire about private chauffeur transportation to Yas Marina Circuit. Pickup and drop-off arrangements are subject to official event traffic management, road closures and access restrictions.

Can the chauffeur wait during the Formula 1 race?

Waiting arrangements can be discussed when you request a quotation. Depending on event regulations, the driver may need to wait at an approved location away from the circuit. Any waiting time or related charges should be agreed in advance.

Do you offer multi-day packages for the Grand Prix weekend?

You can request a customised multi-day chauffeur arrangement covering airport transfers, hotel transportation, race-day journeys and other destinations. Availability and pricing depend on your requested schedule.

Is the chauffeur service price fixed during Formula 1?

We provide a quotation based on your journey and service requirements. The agreed price, including any applicable waiting time, additional hours or other charges, should be confirmed before booking.

Can I book a chauffeur from Dubai to Yas Marina Circuit?

You can request private transportation from Dubai hotels or other locations to the Yas Marina Circuit area. Advance booking is recommended, especially on race day.

Which airport do you provide Formula 1 transfers from?

You can request airport transportation from Zayed International Airport in Abu Dhabi to hotels, Yas Island and other destinations, subject to availability.
