Privacy Policy

This page explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you contact Private Hire Limousine through this website.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you may choose to provide your name, telephone number, email address, pickup and destination details, travel date, passenger count and other information needed to respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information you submit may be used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, discuss journey arrangements and communicate about a requested service.

WhatsApp and email

If you select a WhatsApp or email link, you will use the relevant third-party service. Its own privacy terms and settings apply to information you send through that service.

Sharing and retention

We do not ask you to submit payment-card details through this website. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, provide a requested service or meet applicable obligations.

External websites

This website may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services.

Contact about privacy

For a privacy-related question, email contact@privatehirelimousine.com.

This is general website copy. Review it against the actual business data handling practices and applicable UAE requirements before treating it as a final legal policy.
