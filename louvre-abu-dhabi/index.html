Discover Louvre Abu Dhabi: Art, Culture and Luxury Travel

Explore Abu Dhabi in comfort with Private Hire Limousine

Louvre Abu Dhabi at sunset, Saadiyat Island.

Abu Dhabi is a city where modern luxury meets timeless culture, and few places capture that blend like Louvre Abu Dhabi. Set on Saadiyat Island, this world-renowned museum invites you to explore the story of humanity through remarkable artworks, striking architecture and the stories of civilizations from around the world.

A World of Art and Culture Under One Roof

Louvre Abu Dhabi is the Arab world’s first universal museum. Its galleries bring together artistic and historical treasures from many civilizations, showing how cultures have influenced one another throughout history.

From ancient artefacts to celebrated paintings and contemporary exhibitions, every gallery offers something new. Whether you are an art lover, a history enthusiast, a curious traveller or a family on holiday, there is something here for every age.

The Iconic Dome and the Rain of Light

The museum’s most breathtaking feature is its silver dome, designed by architect Jean Nouvel. Spanning roughly 180 metres, it is made of geometric star patterns arranged in overlapping layers. As sunlight filters through, it creates the mesmerising effect known as the Rain of Light.

Light, shadow, water and architecture combine to create a peaceful atmosphere unlike anywhere else in the city. Stroll the waterfront walkways and take photographs with the Arabian Gulf as your backdrop.

Why Louvre Abu Dhabi Belongs on Your Itinerary

  • Permanent galleries and changing exhibitions
  • Distinctive architecture and waterfront surroundings
  • A Children’s Museum with engaging experiences for younger visitors
  • A refreshing alternative to shopping malls and typical sightseeing

Whether it’s your first visit to Abu Dhabi or your tenth, Louvre Abu Dhabi shows the UAE’s growing reputation as a global cultural destination.

Plan Your Visit

Louvre Abu Dhabi is in the Saadiyat Cultural District on Saadiyat Island. It is accessible by road, with a designated passenger pickup and drop-off point near the main entrance.

  • Book your tickets in advance and check the latest opening hours.
  • Allow about two to three hours for the galleries, plus extra time for the architecture and waterfront.
  • Arrange your transport ahead of time, especially on weekends, holidays and busy tourist periods.
