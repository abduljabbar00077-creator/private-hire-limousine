About Private Hire Limousine

Private Hire Limousine provides a point of contact for private chauffeur and journey enquiries in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Services and journey enquiries

Visitors can enquire about airport transfers, hourly chauffeur hire, hotel pickups, city journeys and private sightseeing arrangements. Each enquiry is reviewed against the requested date, route, vehicle and availability.

How bookings are handled

Customers can share their journey requirements by WhatsApp or email and request a quotation. An enquiry or quotation request is not itself a confirmed booking. The journey is confirmed only after the details and quotation have been accepted.

Contact

For service information or a quotation, visit our Contact page.
