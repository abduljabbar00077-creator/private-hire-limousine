Abu Dhabi continues to establish itself as one of the world’s most exciting destinations for culture, architecture and luxury tourism. Among its most anticipated landmarks is Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, an ambitious museum planned for the beautiful Saadiyat Island.

For visitors who appreciate exceptional experiences, a journey to this destination can become a memorable part of their Abu Dhabi itinerary.

A Museum That Redefines Modern Art

Guggenheim Abu Dhabi is planned to offer much more than a traditional museum visit. Its collection is intended to focus on modern and contemporary art, bringing together diverse artistic perspectives from across the world.

Visitors can look forward to paintings, sculptures, photography, installations, moving images and new media. Artistic voices from West Asia, North Africa and South Asia are expected to be an important part of the museum’s cultural perspective.

Extraordinary Architecture by Frank Gehry

The museum’s distinctive architectural design was created by renowned architect Frank Gehry. Its dramatic sculptural forms and monumental cones are designed to create a striking silhouette against the Arabian Gulf.

The planned design includes interconnected galleries, shaded outdoor spaces, terraces and courtyards, connecting the museum with its coastal surroundings.

Saadiyat Island: Abu Dhabi’s Cultural Destination

Guggenheim Abu Dhabi will form part of the Saadiyat Cultural District. Visitors may plan a cultural day out that also includes Louvre Abu Dhabi, Zayed National Museum and teamLab Phenomena Abu Dhabi. Check each attraction’s official website for current opening details before visiting.
