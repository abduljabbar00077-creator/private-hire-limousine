Discover Guggenheim Abu Dhabi

Explore a new icon of art, architecture and luxury travel on Saadiyat Island.

Abu Dhabi continues to establish itself as one of the world’s most exciting destinations for culture, architecture and luxury tourism. Among its most anticipated landmarks is Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, an ambitious museum planned for Saadiyat Island.

For visitors who appreciate exceptional experiences, a journey to this remarkable destination can become an unforgettable part of an Abu Dhabi itinerary.

A Museum That Redefines Modern Art

Guggenheim Abu Dhabi is planned to offer much more than a traditional museum visit. Its collection is intended to focus on modern and contemporary art, bringing together diverse artistic perspectives from across the world.

Visitors can look forward to paintings, sculptures, photography, installations, moving images and new media. Particular attention is expected to be given to artistic voices from West Asia, North Africa and South Asia, creating opportunities to discover stories and perspectives that connect cultures and generations.

Extraordinary Architecture by Frank Gehry

One of the most striking features of Guggenheim Abu Dhabi is its distinctive architectural design by renowned architect Frank Gehry. Its dramatic sculptural forms and monumental cones are designed to create a memorable silhouette against the Arabian Gulf.

The planned design includes interconnected galleries, shaded outdoor spaces, terraces and courtyards, creating a connection between the museum, its visitors and the surrounding landscape.

Saadiyat Island: Abu Dhabi’s Cultural Destination

Guggenheim Abu Dhabi will form part of the Saadiyat Cultural District, home to important cultural attractions. Visitors can plan a cultural day out that may also include Louvre Abu Dhabi, Zayed National Museum and teamLab Phenomena Abu Dhabi.

Whether travelling as a couple, with family or as part of a private sightseeing trip, Saadiyat Island offers a chance to experience a different side of Abu Dhabi beyond its skyline, shopping destinations and luxury hotels.
