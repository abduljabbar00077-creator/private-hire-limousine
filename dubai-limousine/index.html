Frequently asked questions

How can I book a limousine in Dubai?

Send your pickup and drop-off locations, date, time and vehicle preference through our quote form or WhatsApp. We will reply with a quotation. Your journey is confirmed only after we confirm it and you accept.

Do you provide Dubai International Airport transfers?

You can request a private pickup or drop-off to or from Dubai International Airport. Include your flight number and arrival time. We are an independent company and are not affiliated with the airport.

Can I book a chauffeur for several hours?

Yes. Request hourly chauffeur hire and tell us the number of hours and vehicle you prefer. Availability and the final quotation are confirmed before booking.

Can I travel from Dubai to Abu Dhabi with a private driver?

Yes. Request a private city-to-city transfer and provide both addresses and your preferred departure time. We will reply with a quotation.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the website form?

No. The form sends an enquiry. A booking is confirmed only when we reply with a quotation and you accept it.
